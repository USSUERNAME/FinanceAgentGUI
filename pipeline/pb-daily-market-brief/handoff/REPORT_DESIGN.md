# Report design contract — 2026-07-21 reference

Visual reference: [07.21 리포트](https://app.notion.com/p/3a42aeeccec5812cba12fa8e9514bb7e)

Use this page as a design and information-hierarchy reference only. Do not copy its live values, article text, signed image URLs, or operational queues into the template.

## Fixed visual hierarchy

1. Two-column masthead: `PB RESEARCH / DAILY MARKET INTELLIGENCE` and `AS OF / INTERNAL MONITORING`.
2. Table of contents.
3. `오늘의 결론`: a market-pulse chart, one conclusion callout, then three compact cards for market regime, key variables, and top risk.
4. `데이터 기준과 수집 상태`: timestamp, price as-of date, delay, and connector status.
5. `시장 스코어보드`: four compact cards (breadth, volatility, credit, rates).
6. Evidence sections: prior-hypothesis review, event scenarios, cumulative hypothesis QA, macro chart, ETF charts, news, filings, and follow-ups.
7. A deterministic bibliography and an internal-monitoring disclaimer at the end.

## Content rules

- Every factual item needs its original URL or an explicit `monitoring_only` limitation.
- Display the observation date next to a market value; report date and price date are not interchangeable.
- Keep “observation → interpretation → confirmation condition → invalidation condition” together.
- News/API metadata graded D stays a lead for review, not a confirmed fact.
- Charts are generated artifacts, not inputs from an expiring Notion image URL.
- Sections with no records should say so briefly; never fill them with generic content.
